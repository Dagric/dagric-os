# Dagric VM-recorded 300-video campaign

This is a production ledger for 300 distinct, evidence-backed video concepts: 30 verified product areas × 10 human presentation formats. It is not a folder of finished videos. Every row stays `SCRIPTED_WAITING_FOR_VM_FOOTAGE` until real Dagric VM footage is recorded, edited, listened to, and audited.

## Production rules

- Use the newest tested Dagric ISO and record the actual VM interface.
- Record clean 1920×1080 source footage, then make a separate native vertical composition; do not stretch 16:9 into 9:16.
- Keep measured results tied to their test context. Hardware, firmware, app, and game compatibility must never be universalized.
- Do not show passwords, account details, private files, host notifications, or unrelated browser tabs.
- Use Dagric-owned media and original narration. No downloaded music.
- Do not publish any item while its status says `SCRIPTED_WAITING_FOR_VM_FOOTAGE`.
- Adobe processing is handled in reviewed batches of at most 20 files.

## Files

- `campaign.json` — dialogue, captions, evidence, shot lists, and platform targets.
- `production-ledger.csv` — compact production tracking sheet.
- `vm-capture-plan.csv` — 90 concrete VM shots grouped into 30 reusable source sequences.
