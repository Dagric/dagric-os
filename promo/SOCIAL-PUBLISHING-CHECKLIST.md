# Dagric social publishing gate

Use this gate on the final rendered file, caption, thumbnail, and destination
link. A post is not approved until every required item passes.

## Content rights

- [ ] The video uses genuine continuous Dagric OS footage for every product claim.
- [ ] Every visible document, image, website, sound, and video is owned, licensed,
      or deliberately created as test material.
- [ ] There is no streamed movie, commercial song, game cinematic, reposted social
      content, or unlicensed stock media.
- [ ] Music is absent, original, or commercially licensed for this platform and use.
- [ ] Narration provenance records the model, voice artifact, version/hash, date,
      and applicable license; no real person's voice is cloned or imitated.

## Privacy and authenticity

- [ ] No password, API key, email inbox, browser history, serial number, customer
      record, notification, or host-computer control is visible.
- [ ] The recording does not call the product a VM, ISO, simulation, or mockup.
- [ ] Claims demonstrated in a virtual environment are not presented as proof of
      physical hardware compatibility.
- [ ] Installation footage is used only when installation is the feature being shown.

## Brand and message

- [ ] Dagric is the primary visual subject; third-party logos remain incidental.
- [ ] Product names are used factually without sponsorship, certification, or
      endorsement language.
- [ ] Optional software is described as “available to install,” not “included.”
- [ ] The opening two seconds show a clear action, result, or question.
- [ ] Any opening title overlay disappears within three seconds and blocks no controls.
- [ ] The closing action is specific: follow the channel, try Dagric Free, visit the
      guide, or download from dagric.com.

## Final quality control

- [ ] One person watched the exported file beginning to end with headphones.
- [ ] Motion is continuous and there are no frozen frames, duplicate videos, black
      frames, accidental cuts, overlapping narration, or clipped words.
- [ ] Voice volume is even, speech is understandable, and pacing sounds natural.
- [ ] Text is legible on a phone without permanent captions or banners.
- [ ] The aspect ratio, resolution, codec, and duration match the target platform.
- [ ] Links, prices, release version, and product claims are current on posting day.

## Standard disclosure

Use when third-party software or marks are prominent:

> Dagric OS is an independent product of Impressions Direct 360 LLC. Third-party
> names and marks belong to their respective owners and are shown only to identify
> software or compatibility. No affiliation or endorsement is implied.

Never paste account credentials into this file, captions, render manifests, or
publishing scripts.
