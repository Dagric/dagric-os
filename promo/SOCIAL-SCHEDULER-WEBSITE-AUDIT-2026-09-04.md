# Dagric social scheduler website audit — 2026-09-04

## Access result

Buffer, Later, and Publer were reachable in Chrome, but none of the three account dashboards had an active signed-in session. Their secure login pages were left open for the account owner. No posts, schedules, channel connections, billing settings, or public pages were changed.

## Best Dagric use for each service

### Buffer

- Use **Publish** as the approval-first master queue across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn, Threads, Bluesky, Pinterest, Facebook, X, Mastodon, and Google Business Profile where connected.
- Use **Create** to organize ideas and repurpose one approved Dagric demonstration into platform-specific copy.
- Use **Community** for centralized comment triage and replies.
- Use **Insights** to identify the hooks, features, lengths, and visual profiles that earn the best response.
- Build a branded **Start Page** with Dagric colors, flexible layouts, videos, products, photos, forms, social links, and traffic analytics.
- High-value discovery: Buffer currently advertises a free **MCP connection** on every plan. It can send drafts to Buffer, schedule from a prompt, inspect the upcoming queue, save ideas, work across connected channels, and summarize performance. Buffer still keeps the queue as the review point before publishing.

### Publer

- Use bulk scheduling and the drag-and-drop calendar for the large Dagric video library.
- Create reusable **hashtag groups** for product demos, security, accessibility, migration, compatibility, Linux, and open-source topics.
- Use **recycling** only for evergreen posts that remain accurate; use **recurring posts** for time-limited campaigns with an expiration date.
- Use spintax/variations carefully so recycled copy does not look duplicated.
- Use first/follow-up comments and thread scheduling where supported to move secondary links out of the main caption.
- Build a branded **Linkie** page with a main CTA, custom colors, drag-and-drop layout, banner, embeds, unlimited links, linked social posts, click analytics, SEO metadata, optional custom domain, and email collection.
- High-value discovery: Publer can connect a scheduled post to Linkie so the post appears automatically as a clickable, trackable card. That could turn every Dagric feature video into a direct path to the Free download, documentation, compatibility guide, or Pro page.

### Later

- Use the visual planner for a deliberate mix of wallpapers, features, durations, and campaign themes.
- Use approvals to review Reels, copy, and images before publishing.
- Use the shared inbox for comments and direct messages where the connected network supports it.
- Use analytics and reports to connect campaigns with website traffic and conversions.
- Build **Link in Bio** with custom buttons, backgrounds, text colors, themes, featured media, scrollable banners, linked posts, a featured banner, custom SEO, and Mailchimp email collection.
- High-value discovery: Later supports up to five destination links on each linked Instagram, TikTok, or Snapchat post and advertises custom affiliate-link sales tracking. This can separate Free download, hardware requirements, documentation, Pro, and support destinations without crowding a caption.
- Later also advertises social listening and content-gap discovery, which could guide new Dagric feature videos instead of relying only on generic trend ideas.

## Recommended Dagric architecture

1. **Buffer as the controlled publishing hub:** drafts arrive in one approval queue; Buffer MCP is the best route for future assistant access after the account owner connects it.
2. **Publer for evergreen distribution:** recycle only audited timeless demonstrations, with variations and a minimum cooldown to prevent repetition.
3. **Later for campaign design and conversion analysis:** use its visual planner, Link in Bio, and reporting for launches and longer campaigns.
4. **One canonical destination:** avoid three competing bio pages. Choose one public Dagric link hub, then keep the other two private or redirect them to the canonical page.
5. **Suggested link-hub order:** Try Dagric Free; Watch live footage; Check your hardware; Read the documentation; Compare Free and Pro; Follow Dagric; Contact/support.
6. **Quality safeguard:** upload only files with automated pass plus completed human listening/visual approval. The current 44-video batch has 44 automated passes but zero completed human approvals.
