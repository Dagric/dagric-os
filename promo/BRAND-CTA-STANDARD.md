## Every publishable video

Use one spoken primary action and one short supporting action. The viewer should
understand both without reading a description.

1. Primary: **Try Dagric OS Free at dagric.com.**
2. Supporting: **Follow Dagric OS here for the next live PC test.**

For proof-led or reviewer videos, replace the primary action with:

> Check the evidence and known limits at dagric.com/review.

For hardware-compatibility videos, replace it with:

> Try the live USB on your own PC before installing.

## Visual and editorial gate

- Product visuals must be continuous screen-recorded video from the running
  Dagric operating system or a physical test PC. Still images, snapshot sequences, generated
  product footage, and slideshow motion are not publishable.
- Show only one product-video layer at a time. Do not use a delayed, blurred,
  cropped, or picture-in-picture duplicate of the same desktop.
- A montage may cut between continuous recordings, but each source needs a
  capture receipt and every cut must advance the explanation.
- A real UI action must happen within the first two seconds and at least every
  four seconds after that. Long idle cursor shots fail review.
- Current social replacement policy is **caption-free**: do not burn captions
  into the pixels and do not ship `.srt` or `.vtt` sidecars. Keep a separate
  plain-text narration transcript only for language and pace auditing.
- Do not place banners, badges, borders, labels, progress rails, or calls to
  action over the Dagric screen recording. An optional opening title may sit in
  unused letterbox space and must be completely gone by three seconds. Public
  titles and narration may say `live footage`, but must not expose internal
  capture terms such as `VM`, `virtual machine`, or `ISO`. Keep the closing call
  to action spoken.
- Narration must be understandable at normal phone volume, target -16 LUFS with
  a -1.5 dB true-peak ceiling, and leave enough pause for the UI action to land.
  Use a lighter voice and a conversational 120–180 WPM delivery; do not slow a
  synthetic voice down until it sounds unnaturally deep.
- Synthetic narration must not be described as a human recording. Use each
  platform's synthetic-media disclosure when its rules require one.
- A video is not publishable until automated cadence/freeze/black-frame/audio/
  no-caption checks pass and a person confirms flow, single visual focus,
  natural delivery, accurate wording, no caption overlay, and an understandable
  call to action.
