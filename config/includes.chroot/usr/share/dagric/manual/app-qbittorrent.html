qBittorrentPRO

A BitTorrent client with no advertising and no bundled anything.

On Windows this wasuTorrent
This one is part of Dagric OS Pro. You are reading the manual on the free edition, so it is not installed on this computer. The page is here on purpose — you should be able to see exactly what Pro adds before you decide whether you want it.

What it is

qBittorrent transfers files over BitTorrent. It looks and behaves like the uTorrent people remember from before uTorrent filled with advertising, and it is open source with nothing bundled.

The everyday legitimate use is large open downloads — Linux ISOs, public datasets, Internet Archive material, game patches. What you do with it beyond that is between you and the law where you live.

Five things you will actually do

  1. Open a torrent or magnet link

    Click a magnet link in Firefox and qBittorrent offers to take it, or drag a .torrent file onto the window. It asks where to save before starting.

  2. Check what you downloaded is genuine

    Reputable projects publish a checksum. Verify with sha256sum thefile.iso in a terminal and compare it to the published value. BitTorrent guarantees the pieces match the torrent; the checksum tells you the torrent itself was the real one.

  3. Limit the speed so the house can still use the internet

    Tools → Options → Speed. Set global upload and download caps, and use the alternative-rate scheduler for daytime versus overnight.

  4. Keep seeding, politely

    Set a share-ratio or seeding-time limit in the options so completed downloads stop on their own. Returning at least what you took is the etiquette that keeps the system working.

  5. Bind it to a VPN

    Options → Advanced → Network interface and select your VPN interface. Then, if the VPN drops, qBittorrent stops rather than quietly falling back to your ordinary connection.

One thing that will surprise you

Your address is visible to every peer, by design. BitTorrent works by connecting you directly to strangers, so anyone in the swarm can see your IP address. That is not a flaw to be patched — it is how the protocol functions. Bind it to a VPN as above if that matters to you, and never assume a torrent client is private.

Where to find it

Launcher → qBittorrent.
