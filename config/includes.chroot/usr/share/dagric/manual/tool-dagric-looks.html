Dagric Looks

Change the shape of the desktop — where the taskbar and panels live.

On Windows this wasrearranging the taskbar, without the fear

What it is

Dagric Looks rebuilds the desktop's panels into a chosen shape. Every edition has three:

  • Classic — a taskbar at the bottom, familiar and comfortable.
  • Focus — one tidy bar, nothing in your way.
  • Eleven — a centred taskbar, clean and modern.

Dagric OS Pro adds four more: Horizon (a menu bar up top with a dock below), Command (a side dock), Unity (a side dock with a global menu bar) and Duo (two panels, top and bottom).

Five things you will actually do

  1. Switch layout

    Open it, pick a row, and the panels rebuild in about a second. A small notification confirms which layout was applied.

  2. Change your mind

    Open it again and pick another. Each layout starts by clearing whatever panels exist, so switching is always clean and never accumulates.

  3. Repair a panel you broke by accident

    This is the fix. Dragged a widget into oblivion, deleted the clock, lost the system tray? Pick your layout again and it is rebuilt from scratch.

  4. Combine it with a mood

    Looks changes the shape; Dagric Styles changes the colours. A centred taskbar in deep Midnight, or a classic taskbar in warm Dawn — they are independent.

  5. Write your own layout

    Layouts are .look files: a name, a description, and one line of Plasma's own scripting API. Copy one from /usr/share/dagric/looks/ to ~/.local/share/dagric/looks/, edit the script line, and it appears in the menu next time.

One thing that will surprise you

Switching layout removes your panel customisations, because it rebuilds the panels. Widgets you added by hand, a resized panel, a pinned application — all of it is replaced by the layout's own definition. That is precisely what makes it a reliable repair tool, and it is worth knowing before you switch on a whim after an hour of arranging things.

Your files, settings, wallpaper and colours are untouched. Only the panels are rebuilt.

Where to find it

Dagric Hub → Desktop layout, launcher → Dagric Looks, or dagric-looks.
