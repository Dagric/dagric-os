Gwenview

Look through your photos, and fix the obvious things without a photo editor.

On Windows this wasPhotos, and Windows Photo Viewer

What it is

Gwenview is the image viewer. Double-click a picture anywhere and this opens. It has two halves: a Browse mode showing thumbnails of a folder, and a View mode showing one image large, with the arrow keys moving between them.

It also does the handful of edits people actually want — rotate, crop, resize, red-eye — without loading a 200 MB photo editor to straighten one holiday snap.

Five things you will actually do

  1. Flick through a folder of photos

    Open any one of them and press the left and right arrow keys. The thumbnail strip along the bottom shows where you are; the Browse button returns you to the grid.

  2. Rotate a photo that came out sideways

    Ctrl+R rotates right, Ctrl+Shift+R left. Press Ctrl+S to keep it. Gwenview keeps an undo history for the whole session, so an over-rotation is one Ctrl+Z away.

  3. Crop and resize

    In View mode, open the Edit panel on the right (or use the Plugins/Edit toolbar buttons). Crop offers fixed ratios for prints; Resize takes pixels or a percentage. Use Save As if you want to keep the original untouched.

  4. Run a slideshow on the TV

    Open the folder, then use the full-screen button on the toolbar. The bar that appears in full screen has the slideshow controls on it — play, the interval between pictures, and shuffle.

  5. Compare two images

    In Browse mode, select two or more thumbnails with Ctrl-click and switch to View. Gwenview shows them side by side with synchronised zoom and pan — the fastest way to pick between three near-identical shots.

One thing that will surprise you

There is no photo library, and no import step. Gwenview shows folders, not an album database, so photos off a camera or a phone are just files you copy into Pictures and can move, rename or back up with any other tool. Nothing catalogues them behind your back, nothing uploads them, and nothing will ever tell you that your library needs upgrading.

Where to find it

Double-click any image, or launcher → Photos (also Gwenview).
