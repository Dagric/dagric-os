Music and sound

Sound, headphones and microphones

Getting the audio out of the right speakers and into the right recording.

On Windows this wasthe volume mixer and Sound settings

What it is

Audio on Dagric is handled by PipeWire, a modern engine that replaces the older PulseAudio and JACK systems at once. You mostly meet it through the speaker icon in the system tray.

Click that icon for the volume slider; click the small arrow or open its settings for the full mixer, where every running application has its own volume and its own choice of output device.

Five things you will actually do

  1. Send sound to the TV or the headphones

    Click the speaker tray icon and pick the output device from the list. HDMI outputs appear when a screen is plugged in. Plugging in headphones normally switches automatically.

  2. Turn one app down without turning everything down

    Open the mixer from the tray icon (or System Settings → Audio). Each application gets its own slider, and its own device — so a video call can use your headset while music stays on the speakers.

  3. Pair Bluetooth headphones

    The Bluetooth tray icon → Add New Device, with the headphones in pairing mode. Once connected they appear as an output device. If the microphone on a headset is needed, choose the headset profile in the mixer — it drops the audio quality, which is why it is not the default.

  4. Pick the right microphone

    System Settings → Audio → Recording. Set the default input and its level. Applications that are recording show up here live, so you can see which one is listening.

  5. Fix &ldquo;no sound at all&rdquo;

    Three checks in order: is the right output device selected in the tray menu; is the application muted in the mixer; is the physical volume wheel or mute key on the laptop on. Nine times in ten it is the first.

One thing that will surprise you

Per-application volume is standard here, and it is better than what you had. Windows has a volume mixer too, buried; on Plasma it is one click from the tray, and it remembers each app's level and device between sessions. Set the notification sounds to twenty per cent once and never think about it again.

Where to find it

The speaker icon in the system tray. Full settings: System Settings → Audio. Bluetooth: the Bluetooth tray icon or System Settings → Bluetooth.
