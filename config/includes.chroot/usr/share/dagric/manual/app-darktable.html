darktablePRO

Develop RAW photographs, non-destructively, across a whole shoot.

On Windows this wasAdobe Lightroom
This one is part of Dagric OS Pro. You are reading the manual on the free edition, so it is not installed on this computer. The page is here on purpose — you should be able to see exactly what Pro adds before you decide whether you want it.

What it is

darktable is a RAW photo workflow: it catalogues a shoot in its lighttable view and develops individual frames in its darkroom view. It reads the RAW formats of essentially every camera — CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW, RAF, ORF and the rest.

Nothing you do alters the original file. Every adjustment is stored as a recipe alongside it, so you can change your mind about an edit you made two years ago, and the untouched RAW is always there.

Five things you will actually do

  1. Import a card of photographs

    In lighttable, use import to add a folder. darktable indexes it in place — it does not move or copy your files into a library folder.

  2. Fix exposure and white balance

    Double-click a photo to enter darkroom. The core modules are exposure, white balance (or color calibration), and filmic rgb for pulling detail back out of bright skies and dark shadows.

  3. Apply the same edit to a hundred photos

    Develop one frame, then in lighttable use history stack → copy, select the rest of the shoot, and paste. This is the reason to use a program like this rather than editing photos one at a time.

  4. Cull the shoot quickly

    In lighttable, hover a photo and press 1 to 5 for a star rating, or R to reject. Then filter the view to three stars and up and work only on those.

  5. Export finished files

    Select the photos, open export in the right-hand panel, choose JPEG, a maximum size and a target folder. Presets remember the settings for “for print” and “for the web”.

One thing that will surprise you

Your edits live in a sidecar file, not in the picture. Next to DSC_1234.NEF darktable writes DSC_1234.NEF.xmp. If you copy photos to another machine and leave the little .xmp files behind, your work does not come with them. Copy the whole folder, always — and if a backup tool is filtering by file type, make sure .xmp is on the list.

Where to find it

Launcher → darktable.
