Music and sound

Elisa

Your music collection, with albums, artists and playlists.

On Windows this wasGroove Music and Windows Media Player

What it is

Elisa is the music app: it indexes your Music folder and presents it as albums, artists, genres and tracks, with cover art and playlists. It is deliberately small and deliberately local — there is no streaming service inside it, no account, and nothing about your listening leaves the machine.

Five things you will actually do

  1. Get your music into it

    Put the files in your Music folder and Elisa finds them. If your collection lives elsewhere — another drive, a network share — add that path in Elisa's settings under the indexed folders, and it will scan there too.

  2. Build a playlist

    Drag albums or tracks into the playlist panel on the right, or use the + button on any item. Save it from the playlist menu; Elisa writes standard .m3u files, which any other player can read.

  3. Fix a wrong album or missing cover

    Elisa reads the tags embedded in your files, so the fix belongs in the files. Right-click a track → Show in folder, then edit the tags with a tagging tool (Picard from Discover is the usual choice). Elisa picks up the change on the next scan.

  4. Control it without leaving what you are doing

    The media controls in the system tray, and on the lock screen, work with Elisa. Laptop media keys work too. Hovering the tray applet shows what is playing.

  5. Play a single file quickly

    Double-clicking one MP3 in Dolphin may open mpv rather than Elisa — that is fine, mpv plays audio perfectly. Elisa is for browsing a collection, not for one file.

One thing that will surprise you

If it looks empty, it is not broken — it just has nothing to index yet. Elisa shows music that is in the folders it watches, and on a fresh install that is an empty Music folder. Copy your collection in (the Migration Assistant does exactly this from a Windows partition) and the library fills itself.

Where to find it

Launcher → Music (also Elisa).
