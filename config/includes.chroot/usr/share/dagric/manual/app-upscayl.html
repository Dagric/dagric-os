UpscaylADD-ON

Enlarge and sharpen a small or old photo, using AI that runs on your own machine.

On Windows this wasTopaz Gigapixel, and the “enhance” websites

What it is

Upscayl enlarges images using AI models, recovering plausible detail rather than just making the pixels bigger. It is the right tool for a scanned family photograph, an old avatar, or a logo somebody sent you at 200 pixels wide.

Everything happens on this computer. Nothing is uploaded, there is no account, and it works with the wifi off — which is worth caring about when the pictures are of your family.

Getting it

Two ways in, and they end up at the same app:

  • Dagric OS Pro — open the Dagric Hub and pick the row for it. A terminal window explains what the app is and asks one yes/no question.
  • Any edition — open Discover, search for it, press Install. It comes from Flathub, so turn Flathub on first if you have not: Finish Setup has a tick box for it.

Either route installs it just for your user account, needs no administrator password, and uninstalls cleanly from Discover's Installed page.

Five things you will actually do

  1. Upscale one image

    Select the image, choose an output folder, pick a model, press Upscayl. The first run downloads nothing extra; the models ship with the app.

  2. Pick the right model

    Digital Art for drawings, logos and screenshots. Ultrasharp or Ultramix Balanced for photographs. Try two on the same image and compare — the difference is obvious.

  3. Do a whole folder

    Switch to Batch Upscayl, choose the input folder and an output folder. Leave it running; it is slow by nature.

  4. Compare before and after

    The result view has a slider you drag across the image. Use it — AI upscaling sometimes invents texture that looks wrong on faces, and you want to notice that before you print it.

  5. Give it the GPU

    In settings, select your graphics card if it is offered. On an NVIDIA machine, install the proprietary driver first with Dagric's driver helper; the difference is minutes versus seconds.

One thing that will surprise you

It invents detail, it does not recover it. The extra sharpness in an upscaled photo is the model's best guess at what was probably there. For a family snapshot that is a gift. For anything where accuracy matters — a document, a licence plate, evidence of any kind — the output is a fabrication and should never be treated as a better copy of the original.

Where to find it

After installing: launcher → Upscayl. Before installing: Dagric Hub → Image upscaler — Upscayl, or Discover.
