Skanlite

Scan a paper document or photograph without a complicated office workflow.

On Windows this wasWindows Scan or the printer maker's scan app

What it is

Skanlite controls a connected scanner, previews the page and saves the result as an image. It works with many USB and network multifunction printers supported by the system.

Five things you will actually do

  1. Choose the scanner

    Turn the device on before opening Skanlite. If asked, select the model or network scanner you intend to use.

  2. Preview the page

    Place the paper squarely, choose Preview, and drag the crop rectangle around only the area you need.

  3. Pick a useful resolution

    Use 300 dpi for ordinary documents, 600 dpi for small print or photos, and lower settings only for quick screen copies.

  4. Scan and name it clearly

    Choose Scan, save into Documents or Pictures, and use a name with subject and date instead of scan0001.

  5. Make a PDF

    Open scanned pages in LibreOffice Draw or use the system's Print to PDF route. Check page order and readability in Okular.

If the scanner is missing

Reconnect USB or confirm both devices are on the same network, then close and reopen Skanlite. A printer working does not always mean its scanner side is available.

Where to find it

Launcher → Skanlite or search for scanner.
