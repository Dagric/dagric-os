Dagric Appearance

Every desktop style and layout as a picture. Try one for real; keep it only if you like it.

On Windows this wasPersonalisation, but with an undo

What it is

Dagric Appearance shows every style and every layout as a thumbnail. Click one and the desktop changes immediately and for real — not a preview image, the actual desktop.

Nothing is saved yet. A bar appears at the bottom offering Keep and Revert with a twenty-second countdown. Answer neither — or find that the change made the screen unreadable — and the desktop puts itself back on its own. Nothing becomes permanent without you pressing Keep.

Five things you will actually do

  1. Try something

    Click any tile. Look at the actual desktop behind the window. Press Keep if you like it, Revert if you do not, or do nothing at all and it reverts itself.

  2. Understand the two kinds of tile

    Styles change the mood: colour scheme, accent colour, wallpaper, and window effects like glassy blur. Layouts change the shape: where the taskbar, panels and menus sit. They combine freely.

  3. Recover from a change that went wrong

    You cannot really get stuck: an unanswered change reverts by itself after twenty seconds. If a layout leaves the panel somewhere unhelpful, open Dagric Looks and choose Classic, which rebuilds the panels from scratch.

  4. Add your own

    Styles and layouts are plain text files. Copy one from /usr/share/dagric/styles/ into ~/.local/share/dagric/styles/, edit it, and it appears here next time — with your own thumbnail if you drop a matching PNG into ~/.local/share/dagric/appearance/thumbs/. No root needed.

  5. Use the text menus instead

    Dagric Looks and Dagric Styles are the same catalogue as a simple list. If the graphical gallery cannot start for any reason, it says so and opens the Styles menu rather than failing.

One thing that will surprise you

It is deliberately impossible to lock yourself out with it. The usual risk with theming is a colour scheme that makes text invisible or a layout that hides the menu, at which point you cannot reach the settings to undo it. Here the undo is automatic and on a timer, so the correct way to use this tool is to click everything.

Where to find it

Dagric Hub → Appearance gallery, launcher → Dagric Appearance, or dagric-appearance.
