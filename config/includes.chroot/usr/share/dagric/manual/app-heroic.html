Heroic Games LauncherADD-ON

An independent launcher supporting Epic Games Store, GOG and Amazon Games accounts.

On Windows this wasthe Epic Games Launcher and GOG Galaxy

What it is

Heroic is an independent open-source launcher supporting Epic Games Store, GOG and Amazon Games accounts. It supports Wine and Proton for Windows titles, but compatibility varies by title, hardware, account region and provider changes. The connected stores, accounts and game licenses are separate third-party services.

Getting it

Two ways in, and they end up at the same app:

  • Any Dagric edition — open the Dagric Hub and pick the row for it. A terminal window explains what the app is and asks one yes/no question.
  • Any edition — open Discover, search for it, press Install. It comes from Flathub, so turn Flathub on first if you have not: Finish Setup has a tick box for it.

Either route installs it just for your user account, needs no administrator password, and uninstalls cleanly from Discover's Installed page.

Dagric and Heroic are not official or endorsed by Epic Games, GOG or Amazon. Their names identify optional supported account services; each provider's current terms govern account access, purchases, entitlements and downloads. Games and optional runtimes may present separate publisher or vendor licenses.

Five things you will actually do

  1. Sign in

    Each service uses its own sign-in flow. Follow the current provider page, confirm the domain before entering credentials, and enable account security options offered by that provider.

  2. Install and test

    Pick a game, choose an install folder, and review the selected compatibility layer. Test the title before moving a large library or depending on online features.

  3. Change the Proton or Wine version

    The game's settings page has a Wine version selector, fed by whatever is installed — including builds added by ProtonUp-Qt or the GE-Proton helper.

  4. Review your library and offers

    Store offers and entitlement rules change. Read the provider's current offer terms before claiming or buying content.

  5. Move a game to another drive

    The game's settings offer to move the installation. Doing it this way keeps Heroic's records straight; dragging the folder in Dolphin does not.

One thing that will surprise you

DRM-free does not mean public domain. Some GOG products offer offline installers, but your purchase is still a license governed by the store and publisher terms. Keep lawful backups where offered and verify that an installer still works on your current hardware before relying on it.

Where to find it

After installing: launcher → Heroic Games Launcher. Before installing: Dagric Hub → Heroic — Epic, GOG, Amazon, or Discover.
