Files and getting around

FilelightPRO

Find out where the disk space actually went, in one picture.

On Windows this wasWinDirStat, TreeSize, or Storage settings
This one is part of Dagric OS Pro. You are reading the manual on the free edition, so it is not installed on this computer. The page is here on purpose — you should be able to see exactly what Pro adds before you decide whether you want it.

What it is

Filelight draws your disk as concentric rings: the centre is the folder you scanned, each ring out is a level deeper, and the size of a segment is the size of the folder. A twenty-gigabyte forgotten video is instantly, visually obvious in a way no list of folders ever manages.

Five things you will actually do

  1. Find out why the disk is full

    Open Filelight and scan your home folder. The biggest segment on the first ring is where to look. Click it to zoom in, and keep going until you reach the actual file.

  2. Jump to a file you found

    Right-click a segment → Open Folder and Dolphin opens there. You can also delete from Filelight's right-click menu, though opening the folder first is the safer habit.

  3. Scan a specific folder or drive

    Scan → Scan Folder, or pick a mounted drive from the overview page. Scanning an external drive works the same way.

  4. Skip folders you do not care about

    Settings → Configure Filelight lets you exclude paths and choose whether to cross onto other filesystems — useful for ignoring a mounted backup drive.

  5. Check a folder before you back it up

    Scan the folder you are about to add to a backup set. Discovering the 30 GB of raw camera footage before the first backup run saves a long evening.

One thing that will surprise you

Your home folder is probably not the problem. On a machine that came from Windows, the usual culprits are a hidden .cache folder, an old Downloads pile, or Flatpak runtimes. All three are safe to prune — and BleachBit does the first one properly. Never delete things outside your home folder to make space; use the package tools instead.

Where to find it

Launcher → Disk Space (also Filelight). Also available from Dolphin's Tools menu when installed.
