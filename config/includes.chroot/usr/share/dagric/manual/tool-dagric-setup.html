Finish Setup

The short list of things that cannot honestly be switched on for you.

On Windows this wasthe out-of-box experience, minus the advertising

What it is

Finish Setup is a checklist of three things, and it is short on purpose. Codecs, VPN support, scanners, printer drivers, firmware updates and the security baseline are all already installed and working, so they are not here.

What remains are the items that need either your consent or your hardware:

  • NVIDIA graphics driver — proprietary, so it is never installed without you asking. Off by default.
  • HP printer and scanner support — HP's own driver package, which some HP all-in-ones need for scanning and ink levels. Off by default.
  • Enable Flathub in Discover — adds tens of thousands of applications to the software centre. On by default.

Five things you will actually do

  1. Run it on a new machine

    Tick what applies, press OK. Each selected item opens its own step; the NVIDIA and HP ones open a terminal and ask for your password, and Flathub is instant.

  2. Run it again later

    It is not a one-time wizard. Open it from the Hub any time — when you buy a printer, when you add a graphics card, or when you decide you do want Flathub after all.

  3. Skip everything

    Untick all three and press OK, or just close the window. Nothing happens. The machine is fully usable without any of them.

  4. Understand the NVIDIA option

    It hands over to the driver helper, which detects your card and handles Secure Boot properly. There is one implementation of that logic, not two.

  5. Understand the Flathub option

    It adds Flathub as a source for Discover. It downloads no applications, and it does not change anything already installed — it only widens the catalogue you can search.

One thing that will surprise you

There are only three items, and that is the feature. The list is short because the work was done before the ISO was built. On a new Windows laptop the equivalent screen is long, half of it is advertising, and the items that matter are mixed in with the ones that make somebody money. Here everything that could be finished has been, and what is left is genuinely your decision.

Where to find it

Dagric Hub → Finish setup, launcher → Finish Setup, or dagric-setup.
