Dagric Styles

Change the mood — colours, accent, wallpaper and window effects.

On Windows this wasPersonalisation: Colours, Themes and Background

What it is

Dagric Styles applies a coordinated look in one click: colour scheme, accent colour, wallpaper, and KWin effects such as the glassy blur behind panels. Every edition has four:

  • Daybreak — the Dagric default: bright, blue, calm.
  • High Contrast — black and white, a gold focus ring, and every soft effect switched off. Its colours are measured against WCAG 1.4.6 (7:1) rather than simply named that, and it is in every edition, free included, because nobody should have to pay to read their own screen.
  • Midnight — deep dark, easy on the eyes at night.
  • Dawn — light and airy with a soft warm accent.

Pro adds Classy (dark glass and amber), Neon (electric cyan with every effect on) and Forest (calm green for long working days).

Five things you will actually do

  1. Apply a style

    Pick a row. The desktop changes in a couple of seconds and a notification names what was applied.

  2. Pick your own accent colour

    The Accent color row opens a colour picker. Choose anything; it is applied to buttons, highlights and window decorations across the whole desktop.

  3. Go back to standard Dagric

    The Reset row returns you to Daybreak — the exact look the machine shipped with. Nothing else is disturbed.

  4. Keep the panels where they are

    Styles never touches panel layout. If you have arranged your taskbar the way you like it, changing style is safe. Dagric Looks is the one that rearranges.

  5. Write your own style

    Copy /usr/share/dagric/styles/daybreak.style to ~/.local/share/dagric/styles/ and edit it. That file documents every key: SCHEME, ACCENT, WALLPAPER, BLUR, TRANSLUCENCY and WOBBLY. Leave a key out and that part of the desktop is left alone.

One thing that will surprise you

Everything it does is a stock KDE setting. Nothing is downloaded, nothing is installed, and no theme engine is involved — the tool is a shell script driving Plasma's own commands. That means a style can never break your desktop in a way that System Settings cannot undo, and it means the .style files are readable text you can copy, edit and share.

Where to find it

Dagric Hub → Colors and mood, launcher → Dagric Styles, or dagric-style.
