HandBrakePRO

Convert video into something that will actually play on the other device.

On Windows this wasHandBrake on Windows, and every dubious online converter
This one is part of Dagric OS Pro. You are reading the manual on the free edition, so it is not installed on this computer. The page is here on purpose — you should be able to see exactly what Pro adds before you decide whether you want it.

What it is

HandBrake converts video from one format to another and, more usefully, makes big files smaller. Feed it a 12 GB camcorder file and get back a 900 MB MP4 that plays on a phone, a TV or a web page.

Five things you will actually do

  1. Shrink a video

    Open the file, choose the preset Fast 1080p30 from the preset list, pick where to save, press Start. That preset is the right answer roughly nine times in ten.

  2. Make something play on an old TV or phone

    The preset list has device categories. Picking the matching one sets the codec, resolution and profile so the device will accept it, which is usually the actual problem rather than file size.

  3. Convert a batch overnight

    Set up the first file, press Add to Queue, then open the next and repeat. Start Queue works through them all.

  4. Keep the subtitles

    The Subtitles tab. Burn in paints them permanently into the picture — necessary for devices that cannot display subtitle tracks; otherwise leave them as a selectable track.

  5. Trade quality against size deliberately

    The Video tab has a constant-quality slider. Lower numbers mean better quality and bigger files; 20–23 is the normal range for H.264. Change one thing at a time and compare.

One thing that will surprise you

It will not copy a commercial DVD or Blu-ray. HandBrake can read an unencrypted disc, but the copy protection on shop-bought discs needs a decryption library that Debian does not ship, for legal reasons that vary by country. Home-made discs and camcorder files are entirely fine. This is a licensing boundary, not a missing feature, and Dagric will not pretend otherwise.

Where to find it

Launcher → HandBrake.
