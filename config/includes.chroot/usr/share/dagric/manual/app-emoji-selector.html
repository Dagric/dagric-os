Emoji Selector

Search, copy and insert emoji and symbols in almost any app.

On Windows this wasthe emoji panel (Windows + .)

What it is

The Emoji Selector is a searchable catalogue of emoji. Picking one copies it so you can paste it into a message, document, filename or browser field. The receiving app decides how the character looks.

Five things you will actually do

  1. Search by meaning

    Type a simple word such as heart, warning or camera, then choose a result.

  2. Browse a category

    Use the category list for faces, food, travel, objects, symbols and flags when you do not know the name.

  3. Paste the selection

    Return to the destination and press Ctrl + V. If nothing appears, that app or font may not support the character.

  4. Reuse a recent emoji

    Open the recent section instead of searching again. Recents remain on this computer.

  5. Use a symbol carefully

    Emoji in passwords, commands and filenames can be hard to type on another device. Prefer ordinary characters there.

Where to find it

Launcher → Emoji Selector. You can also search the launcher for the word emoji.
