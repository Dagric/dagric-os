KdenlivePRO

Multi-track video editing: cut, title, colour and export.

On Windows this wasWindows Movie Maker, and much of Premiere Pro
This one is part of Dagric OS Pro. You are reading the manual on the free edition, so it is not installed on this computer. The page is here on purpose — you should be able to see exactly what Pro adds before you decide whether you want it.

What it is

Kdenlive is a proper non-linear video editor: as many video and audio tracks as you like, keyframed effects, titles, transitions, colour correction, and export presets for YouTube and the rest.

It is the editor to reach for first on this machine. If you need studio colour grading or an ecosystem of plug-ins, DaVinci Resolve is a free download away — but most projects never need it.

Five things you will actually do

  1. Start a project at the right settings

    When Kdenlive asks for a project profile, match your footage — 1080p at 30 frames per second for most phone video, 4K if that is what you shot. Getting this right at the start avoids rendering surprises at the end.

  2. Cut a clip

    Drag footage from the Project Bin to the timeline. Position the playhead and press Shift+R to razor the clip under it, then select the unwanted piece and press Delete. Hold Shift while deleting to close the gap.

  3. Add a title

    Project → Add Title Clip. Type, style, position, then drag it onto a track above your video. Its length on the timeline is how long it stays on screen.

  4. Fix shaky or dark footage

    The Effects panel: Stabilise for shake (it analyses first, which takes a while), and Color Correction or Lift/Gamma/Gain for exposure. Drag an effect onto a clip and adjust it in the Effect Stack panel.

  5. Export the finished film

    Project → Render. Pick MP4-H264/AAC for something that plays everywhere, choose a file name, and press Render to File. It runs in the background so you can carry on working.

One thing that will surprise you

Turn on proxy clips before you start, not after. Project → Project Settings → Proxy clips makes Kdenlive edit against small, low-quality copies and then render the final film from the originals. On 4K or phone footage it is the difference between a responsive timeline and a slideshow. Nearly every “Kdenlive is slow” complaint is this setting.

Save often, too. It is stable but it is a video editor.

Where to find it

Launcher → Kdenlive, or search video editor.
