Internet and communication

KDE ConnectPRO

Your phone and this computer, talking to each other over your own Wi-Fi.

On Windows this wasPhone Link (Your Phone)
This one is part of Dagric OS Pro. You are reading the manual on the free edition, so it is not installed on this computer. The page is here on purpose — you should be able to see exactly what Pro adds before you decide whether you want it.

What it is

KDE Connect pairs your phone with this computer over the local network. Phone notifications appear on the desktop, files move both ways by drag and drop, the clipboard can be shared, the phone becomes a remote control or a slide clicker, and you can find a mislaid handset by making it ring.

Nothing goes through anybody's server. The two devices talk directly to each other on your own network, which is why it also works when the internet is down.

Five things you will actually do

  1. Pair the phone

    Install KDE Connect from the Play Store or the App Store, put both devices on the same Wi-Fi, open the app, and each side offers to pair with the other. Accept on both. Pairing happens once.

  2. Send a file to the phone

    Right-click the file in Dolphin → Send to → your phone. Or drag it onto the phone's entry in the KDE Connect window. Going the other way, share to KDE Connect from the phone.

  3. Read and reply to phone notifications

    Enable notification access in the phone app. Messages then appear in Plasma's notification area, and on Android you can reply straight from the desktop.

  4. Use the phone as a presentation remote

    The Slideshow remote plugin in the phone app moves through slides in Impress or a PDF, with a virtual laser pointer.

  5. Share the clipboard

    Turn on clipboard sync in the plugin list. Copy a link on the phone and paste it here. It is the single most-used feature once you have it.

One thing that will surprise you

If pairing does not find your phone, it is almost always the firewall zone. KDE Connect needs a range of ports open, and Dagric opens them only on networks you have marked as Home — never on café or airport Wi-Fi, because that range is a pairing surface that has no business being reachable from strangers. Open System Settings → Firewall and set the current connection's zone to home, and the phone appears.

Where to find it

Launcher → Connect Your Phone (also KDE Connect); texting is a second entry, Phone Messages. Once paired it also lives in the system tray and in Dolphin's Send to menu.
