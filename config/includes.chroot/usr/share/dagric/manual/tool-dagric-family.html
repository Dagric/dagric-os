Family Limits

Set daily screen time and bedtime for another person's account.

On Windows this wasMicrosoft Family Safety screen-time limits

What it is

Family Limits applies a daily allowance and optional bedtime to a family member's local account. It does not read messages, record the screen or upload activity. It will not let you lock the only administrator account.

Before you start

Create a separate standard account for the family member and ask them to sign in once. Keep your administrator account separate so you can change or remove limits later.

Five things you will actually do

  1. Choose the person

    Select a known local account. Service accounts are hidden and the only administrator is protected.

  2. Set daily time

    Turn on the limit and choose a realistic total. Explain the rule to the person using the account.

  3. Set bedtime

    Choose when access stops and resumes. Set both hours the same when you want no bedtime restriction.

  4. Choose what happens

    Lock preserves open work; Sign out closes applications and can lose unsaved changes. Lock is the safer default.

  5. Remove limits

    Select the same person, choose Remove all limits, save, and have them sign in again.

If the person is missing

Have them sign in once, sign out, then reopen Family Limits. The screen-time service creates its per-person record on that first session.

Where to find it

Launcher → Family Limits. Saving asks for an administrator password.
