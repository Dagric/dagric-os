# Dagric community code of conduct

Dagric exists to give people more control over their computers. The project
community should offer that same sense of safety and ownership.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas, code, documentation, and test results—not people.
- Welcome beginners and explain assumptions that experienced Linux users may
  take for granted.
- Protect privacy. Never post another person's credentials, private logs,
  identifying information, or support records.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and do not misrepresent affiliations,
  certifications, benchmarks, or test results.
- Report security vulnerabilities privately instead of publishing exploitation
  details in a public issue.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate
misinformation, spam, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not accepted in
Dagric spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock conversations, reject
contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
necessary to protect the community. Decisions should be proportional,
documented privately, and applied consistently.

Report conduct concerns privately through https://dagric.com/contact or
support@dagric.com. Include links or screenshots and enough context to review
the event. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, Discussions, project
events, support channels, and people representing Dagric in public spaces.
