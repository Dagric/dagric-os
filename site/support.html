Help is built in.

Most answers are already on your machine — and when they aren't, a human reads every message.

First stops on your computer

The offline guide

Press Meta, type Guide, and open Dagric Welcome & Guide — printers, Wi-Fi, apps, backups, and shortcuts, readable even with no internet.

The Dagric Hub

One window for the fix-it tools: graphics drivers, Finish Setup (printers/HP), Security Checkup, and the layout switcher. Meta → type Hub.

Something broke after an update?

Reboot, and in the boot menu open Dagric snapshots — pick the snapshot from before the update and you're back in minutes, files intact.

Common quick fixes

  • No sound after boot — click the speaker icon in the tray and check the output device; a monitor's HDMI audio often grabs default.
  • Wi-Fi missing on very new laptops — connect briefly by phone-USB tethering or cable, run updates (firmware arrives via LVFS), reboot.
  • NVIDIA screen issues — open the Hub → Graphics drivers; with Secure Boot on, complete the blue MOK screen at reboot (Enroll MOK → Continue).
  • A Windows program you miss — on Pro, open the Hub → Run Windows apps (Bottles), or use the web app in Firefox.
  • Printer won't appear — most work driverless; for HP all-in-ones run the Hub → Finish Setup → HP support.

Reach a human

Email repo@dagric.com with what you were doing, what happened, and (if you can) a photo of the screen. Pro customers: replying to your Stripe receipt also reaches us, and includes your purchase details automatically — that's the fastest route.

Include the output of cat /etc/os-release (press Meta, type Konsole, paste that line) so we know your exact build.
