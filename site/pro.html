Bottles + Wine Also free

Run Windows software in tidy per-app containers tuned for compatibility — one click from the Dagric Hub, on either edition. The Pro half is underneath it: Wine 10.0 and winetricks preinstalled, plus 32-bit Wine for classic .exe installers. The free edition ships no Wine at all.

Steam + Proton Also free

Windows in a window Pro only

This one genuinely is Pro: the hypervisor, virt-manager and the TPM emulator are not on the free edition, which is why the Hub hides the row there rather than offering a button that can't work.

Don't buy Pro if…

Four reasons to keep your $39. We would rather lose the sale than take it from somebody who was going to want it back.

…you only wanted Steam, Heroic, Bottles or Ollama

All four are one click from the Hub on the free edition, and so are ProtonUp-Qt, Proton-GE, ONLYOFFICE, Joplin, LocalSend, Cryptomator and Upscayl. If that list is your whole reason for buying, download the free ISO instead — you will get the same buttons. Pro's gaming value is the 32-bit Vulkan, GameMode and MangoHud underneath them, which matters if you play, and not otherwise.

…the disk is nearly full

Pro needs 40 GB of free space against the free edition's 25 GB, because the creative suite, the Wine and gaming runtimes and the CJK fonts are all really there on the disk. On a small SSD or an older laptop that is a real constraint, and it will not get better after you have paid.

…you are comfortable with a package manager

Every application Pro preinstalls — GIMP, Krita, Inkscape, Blender, Kdenlive, OBS, darktable, Audacity, KeePassXC and the rest — is free software, from Debian's own archive, under its own licence. You can install every one of them on the free edition yourself, for nothing, and we would not think less of you. What the $39 buys is that somebody already did it, wired the pieces together, and tested that the result works on first boot.

…you need DaVinci Resolve preinstalled

No operating system can legally ship it — Blackmagic's licence forbids redistribution, so the Hub walks you through their own download, on both editions equally. Buying Pro does not get Resolve installed for you. Kdenlive is preinstalled and is a real video editor; if it isn't the one you need, that is worth knowing before checkout rather than after.
