July 24, 2026 · Update

More Looks, and a curated open-source toolkit

Dagric Looks grows to seven layouts. New this update:

  • Eleven (free) — a centered taskbar in the Windows-11 style.
  • Unity (Pro) — a side dock with a global menu bar up top.
  • Duo (Pro) — two panels, top and bottom, for power users.

Pro also gains a hand-picked set of open-source apps that make "own it outright" real — KeePassXC (passwords in a local vault), Syncthing (device-to-device sync with no cloud), Flatseal (control every app's permissions), darktable (RAW photography), Audacity and HandBrake (audio & video), plus qBittorrent and Filelight. And a one-click LocalSend helper for private, cross-platform file sharing — like AirDrop, without the cloud. See it all on the Pro page.

July 23, 2026 · Update

Looks, the Hub, and a store

The biggest update yet to Dagric OS 1.0 “Foundation” — the one that turns it from a project into a product:

  • Dagric Looks — switch your desktop layout in one click. Classic and Focus on the free edition; Horizon (top bar + dock) and Command (side dock) on Pro. Every layout keeps the Dagric branding and your files untouched.
  • The Dagric Hub — one window gathering every owner tool: drivers, printers, the Security Checkup, layouts, guides, and Pro's one-click installers.
  • Branding, everywhere — the D monogram now carries through the boot splash, login, wallpaper, launcher button, and system pages.
  • Accessibility that actually speaks — the Orca screen reader now ships with its speech engine. If you can't see the screen, Dagric talks.
  • Honest Windows-app support (Pro) — Bottles joins Wine, plus one-click Steam, Heroic (Epic/GOG/Amazon), and ProtonUp-Qt. Nothing proprietary is bundled — everything installs only when you ask.
  • NVIDIA + Secure Boot, solved — the driver helper now enrolls the signing key and walks you through the reboot screen. No more black screens.
  • Print-to-PDF, L2TP VPNs, encrypted-boot plumbing, and a long list of audited fixes.

Alongside the release: this website, the Pro edition store with Stripe checkout and a 14-day refund promise, and GPG-signed downloads on the download page.

July 21, 2026 · Announcement

Introducing Dagric OS: own it outright

Dagr — Old Norse for day. Ric — ruler. Dagric: your computer, your day, your rule.

Dagric OS is a fast, private desktop operating system built on Debian 13 and KDE Plasma — debloated, hardened, and finished. No telemetry exists in it. No ads, no accounts, no forced restarts, no subscriptions. The free edition is a complete daily driver, forever; Pro adds the creative studio, gaming and Windows apps, the developer stack, and local AI for a one-time $39.

Version 1.0 "Foundation" ships with UEFI + Secure Boot support, btrfs snapshots with boot-menu rollback, a first-run welcome, and an offline guide. It was built and tested in the open: every release publishes signed checksums, and everything we add is open source.

Try it from a USB stick — your disk stays untouched until you choose otherwise.
