No accounts, no ads

Private AI, if you want AI

Security you don't have to assemble

Hardened by default

AppArmor mandatory access control active, hardened kernel settings, firewall on — from the first boot, before you change a thing.

Updates on your terms

Security patches install silently in the background. The machine never restarts itself.

Verified releases

Every ISO ships SHA-256 checksums signed with our GPG key, so you can prove your download is untouched.

Secure Boot, supported

Boots with Secure Boot on, with no firmware settings to change. Installing the NVIDIA driver? Dagric enrolls the signing key and walks you through the blue MOK screen at the next reboot — the step that otherwise ends in a black screen. (Tested in UEFI virtual machines; see Security.)

Security Checkup

One click runs a Lynis audit and shows your hardening score — security you can see.

Opt-in lockdown (Pro)

OpenSnitch application firewall, plus USB Protection: one click remembers the devices you actually use and blocks malicious USB impostors — without ever locking out your own keyboard.
