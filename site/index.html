Questions, answered

Is Dagric OS really free?

Yes. The free edition is a complete, private, hardened desktop — free forever, no strings. Pro adds the creative, gaming, developer, and Windows-app suites for a one-time $39.

Will my Windows files and drives work?

Yes. NTFS and exFAT drives mount out of the box, your Office documents open with matching fonts, and Pro runs many Windows programs directly via Bottles and Wine.

Is it a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time purchase. There are no license servers and no activation — you own your copy outright, with free updates.

What if I buy Pro and don't like it?

Reply to your receipt within 14 days and we refund in full — no quiz, no hoops. Details in the terms & refunds.

What about my privacy?

Dagric OS ships zero telemetry. There is nothing to opt out of because nothing is collected — no accounts, no ads, no “diagnostics.”

Can I try it without installing?

Yes — every ISO is also a live system. Boot it from USB and use the full desktop without touching your disk; install only when you're ready.

What if an update breaks something?

Dagric snapshots the system before updates. Pick “Dagric snapshots” in the boot menu and you're back to the moment before — files intact.

Is the software open source?

Yes — Dagric OS is built from open-source software on a Debian base, and GPL source availability is honored. Proprietary extras like Steam or the NVIDIA driver are never bundled; they install only when you ask, with one click.
