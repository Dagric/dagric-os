The promise

Zero telemetry

Nothing phones home. There is no toggle to find because there is nothing to turn off.

No ads, no accounts

Your desktop is not a billboard, and your computer works without asking anyone's permission.

Updates, your way

Security patches install silently in the background. The machine never restarts itself.

Secure by default

AppArmor confinement, a hardened kernel, and a firewall out of the box — before you change a thing. Pro adds an opt-in application firewall (OpenSnitch) and USB device controls (USBGuard) you switch on when you want them.

Feels familiar

Taskbar, launcher, and tray where you expect them. Your NTFS drives and USB sticks just work.
