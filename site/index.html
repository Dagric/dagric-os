Questions, answered

Is Dagric OS really free?

Yes. The free edition is a complete, private, hardened desktop — free forever, no account. Pro adds the creative, gaming, developer, and Windows-app suites for a one-time $39.

Why pay $39 when other Linux is free?

You don't have to — the free edition is a real, complete distro. Pro is one curated, tested, supported product (the Hub, Looks, snapshot-rollback, signed releases, Windows-app support, human support) for people who'd rather not assemble and harden a general distro themselves. The tools are open source; the $39 buys the craftsmanship.

Will it run on my PC?

Almost certainly, if it's a 64-bit Intel or AMD machine from roughly 2012 onward with 4 GB of RAM (8 GB recommended) and 25 GB of free disk — 40 GB for Pro. UEFI with Secure Boot and older BIOS machines both work. And you don't have to take our word for it: every Dagric USB stick puts Check This PC on the live desktop, right beside the installer. It reports in plain language on your Wi-Fi and network, graphics, sound, Secure Boot and drives — before anything is installed, without changing a thing. Get a stick going →

Will my Windows files and drives work?

Yes. NTFS and exFAT drives mount out of the box, your Office documents open with matching fonts, and both editions run many Windows programs through Bottles — one click from the Hub — with Pro adding the tuned Wine runtime underneath.

Can I keep Windows and dual-boot?

Yes — choose Install alongside in the installer and pick how much space each system gets. You choose which to boot at startup.

Is it a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time purchase for one machine, verified once when you claim it — nothing phones home afterwards. You own your copy outright, with free updates.

What if I buy Pro and don't like it?

Reply to your receipt within 14 days and we refund in full — no quiz, no hoops. Details in the terms & refunds.

Is the software open source?

Yes — built from open-source software on a Debian base, and every release is GPG-signed and checksummed so you can verify it. Proprietary extras like Steam or the NVIDIA driver are never bundled; they install only when you ask.
